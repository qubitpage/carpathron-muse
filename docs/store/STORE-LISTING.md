# Meta Horizon Store listing — Carpathron Avatar

**Name:** Carpathron Avatar
**Short description (≤ 250 chars):** Become your robot. See through its camera, drive it with your controllers and turn your head to turn it. Talk to Meta Muse AI to look around, map the room and ask what it sees.

**Long description:**
Carpathron Avatar puts you inside your robot.

• SEE WHAT IT SEES — the robot's live camera fills a big screen in front of you.
• DRIVE NATURALLY — thumbsticks drive the left and right wheels (or one-stick arcade), trigger for turbo, A/X to stop instantly.
• TURN YOUR HEAD, THE ROBOT TURNS — head steering keeps the camera pointed where you look.
• TALK TO MUSE — hold the grip and speak: "drive forward two seconds", "look behind", "scan the room". Ask "what do you see?" and Meta Muse answers from the robot's camera.
• ROOM MAPPING — the robot turns a full circle and builds a map of what is around it.
• WORKS WITH ANY CARPATHRON ROBOT — robots that speak the open Carpathron Robot Protocol are found on your Wi-Fi automatically (the reference robot is an ESP32-CAM 4WD car; firmware and guide on GitHub).

Requires a Carpathron-compatible robot on the same Wi-Fi. Voice and AI features use the free Muse Carpathron app for Windows with a Meta Model API key or Meta account.

**Input:** Touch controllers

**Privacy policy:** https://github.com/msrusu87/carpathron/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md

**Permissions justification**
- `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`: push-to-talk voice commands, only while the user holds the right grip.
- `android.permission.INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`: connect to the user's robot and PC on the local network.

**Data Use Checkup:** no data collected by the developer; audio and images are processed on the user's own PC and by the Meta Model API at the user's request.
