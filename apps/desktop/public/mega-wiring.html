Baby Carpathron · KUONGSHUN AD174 + Arduino Mega 2560

Carpathron car wiring

The ESP32-CAM keeps the camera, Wi-Fi and motors. The Mega becomes the sensor brain and sends its readings to the ESP32 over two wires. Everything below uses only the parts on your table: jumper wires, two resistors, no soldering.

Your Mega, as it lies on the table

Lay the Mega like in your photo: USB and power jack at the bottom, POWER and ANALOG pins down the right side, pins 0–21 down the left side, the long double row at the top. Each coloured ring is a pin you use. Its number matches the same number on the sensor below. Orange rings mark the 6 holes the insert's pins go through.

ESP32-CAM board

Its right-hand pin column, camera lens at the top. Top to bottom:

  • 3V3not used
  • 3V3not used
  • 16not used
  • 0not used
  • GND2Mega GND, left side, just above AREF
  • 5V1Mega 5V, top double row, upper pin at the far LEFT (edge)
  • RXD3through 1 kΩ to Mega TX1 18
  • TXD4Mega RX1 19
  • GNDnot used
  • GND3b2 kΩ up to the RXD wire (see below)

The two resistors on wire 3: Mega pin 18 → 1 kΩ → ESP32 RXD. At the ESP32 end, a 2 kΩ goes from that same RXD pin to the bottom GND pin. Two 1 kΩ in a row make the 2 kΩ. This turns the Mega's 5V signal into 3.3V so the ESP32 is safe.

HC-SR04, the eyes

Pins along the bottom edge, left to right:

  • VCC5Mega 5V, top double row, lower pin at the far LEFT
  • Trig6Mega 22, lower row, second from the LEFT
  • Echo7Mega 23, upper row, second from the LEFT
  • GND8Mega GND, top double row, upper pin at the far RIGHT

The two round transducers push into the eye holes of the face panel.

MPU-6050 (GY-521)

Pins along one edge:

  • VCC9Mega 3.3V, right side
  • GND10Mega GND, right side, the GND next to 5V
  • SCL11Mega SCL 21, left side, top pin
  • SDA12Mega SDA 20, left side
  • XDA · XCL · AD0 · INTnot used

Lay it flat. The printed X arrow points to the front of the car.

DHT11 module

Three pins. Check the letters printed on your module:

  • S13Mega A0, right side, A0 at the bottom of the analog pins
  • + (middle)14Mega 5V, right side
  • −15Mega GND, right side, the GND next to Vin

Put it by a side vent so it measures the room, not the motor driver.

Which jumper wire for each

  • Male–female (pin on one end, socket on the other) for almost everything: the pin end goes into the Mega's black sockets, the socket end onto the sensor's pins or the ESP32 board's pins.
  • Wires 1, 2, 4 (Mega ↔ ESP32 board): male–female, pin end in the Mega, socket end on the ESP32 board's right-hand pins.
  • Wire 3 with the resistors: push one leg of a 1 kΩ straight into Mega socket 18 (no wire needed). Twist its other leg together with one leg of the 2 kΩ (two 1 kΩ twisted end to end), push both into the socket end of a female–female wire, and put its other end on the ESP32's RXD. The 2 kΩ's free leg goes into another female–female wire to the ESP32's bottom GND. Tape over the twisted legs.
  • Keep the wires short and bundle them with tape; a dab of hot glue on a plug stops it falling off when the car drives.

What needs soldering

  • MPU-6050: yes. Its 8 pins came loose. Solder the pin strip into the board (short side through the holes, long side down), at least VCC GND SCL SDA. A loose strip gives a gyro that drops out while driving.
  • HC-SR04 and DHT11: no, their pins are already soldered.
  • Mega and ESP32 board: no, the jumpers plug straight into their sockets and pins.
  • Mega proto shield: not needed. Leave it in the box.
  • No soldering iron tonight? Push the strip into the MPU-6050's holes slightly tilted so every pin presses against its hole, then fix it with hot glue. It works for a demo, but solder it later.

Not used tomorrow

  • SG90 servo: the eyes are fixed in the head, so nothing needs turning. Later it could turn the whole head (signal on pin 9).
  • 28BYJ-48 stepper + driver board: it draws too much current from the Mega and the car doesn't need it.
  • The second accelerometer board (GND VCC CS INT SDO SDA SCL): the MPU-6050 already does its job.
  • Mega proto shield: optional. Solder it only if you want the PIR or a tidier build. It plugs on top and passes every pin through.

Before you switch on

  1. Check every wire against the table, especially 5V and GND never swapped and nothing on VIN.
  2. The 2 kΩ goes from ESP32 RXD to GND. Without it, the Mega's 5V goes straight into the ESP32.
  3. When you upload code to the Mega over USB, switch the car off and pull the red 5V jumper, so the PC and the car don't both feed 5V.
  4. Update the ESP32 over Wi-Fi from Muse ("Update car firmware"), not over USB, because its USB serial pins now talk to the Mega.
  5. If the camera restarts when the headlight is on, the car's 5V is overloaded. Move the Mega to the battery: battery + after the switch to VIN, battery − to GND.

Changed from my earlier note: the MPU-6050 now goes on the Mega, not the ESP32. RXD and TXD are the only two free pins on the ESP32, and the Mega link needs both. The Mega sends the heading and the distance to the ESP32, so exact turns and the obstacle stop keep working, with the HC-SR04 as the distance sensor. This needs a Mega program and a small ESP32 firmware update.
