# Privacy policy — Carpathron Avatar and Muse Carpathron

_Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Publisher: Qubitpage Limited (Carpathron)_

Carpathron apps run **on your own devices and your own local network**. We, the developer, do not run servers
for these apps, do not collect analytics and do not receive your data.

## Carpathron Avatar (Meta Quest app)

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Robot camera video and photos** | Streamed directly from your robot to your headset over your local Wi-Fi. Photos you take are stored on the headset. |
| **Microphone (only while you hold the right grip)** | The recording is sent over your local network to **your own PC** running Muse Carpathron, which sends it to the **Meta Model API (Muse Voice Transcribe)** to turn it into text. Nothing is recorded otherwise. |
| **"What do you see?"** | Your PC takes a photo from the robot and sends it with your question to the **Meta Model API (Muse Spark)** for an answer. |
| **Network discovery** | The app looks for robots and for Muse Carpathron on your local network only. |
| **Link token** | When you approve the headset on your PC, a random token is stored on the headset and on your PC. |

## Muse Carpathron (Windows app)

Chats, memories, projects, photos, room maps and settings are stored **on your PC**. Messages, images and voice
notes you send to the AI are processed by **Meta** through the Meta Model API or Muse Code under Meta's own
terms and privacy policy. If you connect WhatsApp, messages flow between WhatsApp and your PC.
Your API key never leaves your PC except to authenticate with Meta.

## Third parties

- **Meta Platforms** (Meta Model API / Muse Code): https://www.meta.com/privacy/
- **WhatsApp** (only if you connect it): https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13.

## Your choices

You can delete photos, maps, chats and memories at any time in the apps or from their folders, unlink the headset by
removing it in Muse Carpathron, and uninstall the apps to remove all local data.
