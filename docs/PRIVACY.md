# Privacy policy — Carpathron Robotics & Coding and Carpathron Avatar

_Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Publisher: Qubitpage LIMITED (Romania) · contact@qubitpage.com_

Carpathron apps run **on your own devices and your own local network**. We, the developer, do not run servers for
these apps, do not collect analytics or telemetry, show no ads, and never receive your data.

## Carpathron Robotics & Coding (Windows app, formerly "Muse Carpathron")

**Stored on your PC only.** Chats, memories, projects, knowledge files, photos, room maps, settings, saved servers
and paired devices are stored in your Windows user folders (`%APPDATA%\Muse Carpathron` and
`%USERPROFILE%\MuseCarpathron`). Passwords, keys and tokens (Meta API key, SSH passwords and keys, GitHub token,
WhatsApp tokens, the app's access key) are encrypted with your Windows account (DPAPI). Paired phones and headsets
are stored as fingerprints only.

**Sent to Meta to get answers.** When you send a message, the text and anything you attach or the agent includes for
that request (files you attached, file contents it read for the task, screenshots of your screen when you let it use
computer use, robot or webcam photos, voice notes) is sent to **Meta** through the Meta Model API or Muse Code and is
processed under Meta's terms and privacy policy. The Activity page lists every outgoing call.

**Only when you use these features:**

| Feature | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Computer use** | Screenshots of your desktop are taken to let the AI see and operate apps; they are sent to Meta with that request. In Ask mode every screenshot needs your approval. A banner shows while it works. |
| **Webcam / phone camera** | Used only to take a robot photo you or the agent asked for. |
| **Remote servers (SSH)** | Commands and files go directly between your PC and your servers. |
| **GitHub** | Repositories, issues and releases go directly between your PC and GitHub with your token. |
| **Web search / web pages** | Pages are fetched from the sites concerned; searches go to Meta search grounding or DuckDuckGo. |
| **MCP servers** | Tools you add run on your PC (or the remote URL you configure) and receive the data you send them. |
| **WhatsApp** | Messages flow between WhatsApp/Meta and your PC. The Microsoft Store version uses only the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. |
| **Phones and Quest on your network** | Off by default. When you switch it on and pair a device with a PIN, that device can use the app over your local Wi-Fi only. |

## Carpathron Avatar (Meta Quest app)

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Robot camera video and photos** | Streamed directly from your robot to your headset over your local Wi-Fi. Photos you take are stored on the headset. |
| **Microphone (only while you hold the right grip)** | The recording is sent over your local network to **your own PC** running Carpathron Robotics & Coding, which sends it to the **Meta Model API (Muse Voice Transcribe)** to turn it into text. Nothing is recorded otherwise. |
| **"What do you see?"** | Your PC takes a photo from the robot and sends it with your question to the **Meta Model API** for an answer. |
| **Network discovery** | The app looks for robots and for the PC app on your local network only. |
| **Link token** | When you approve the headset on your PC, a random token is stored on the headset; the PC keeps only its fingerprint. |

## Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13.

## Your choices

You can delete chats, memories, photos and maps in the app or from the folders above, remove paired devices in
*Settings → Phone & Quest*, disconnect GitHub and WhatsApp in Settings, and uninstall the apps. Uninstalling keeps
your data folders so a reinstall finds them; delete the folders above to remove everything.
